Why medicine + geography, and what I’m building right now

I’m interested in medicine, but I’ve always cared about context: where people live, what resources exist, and how systems shape outcomes. That’s why global health and medical geography make sense to me alongside biology.

Patient-centred care Equity Systems thinking Community health

What I value

Good healthcare is not only about knowing the science. It’s also communication, trust, consistency, and understanding the barriers that people face when they try to access care.

Clinical mindset

  • Professionalism, accuracy, and patient privacy
  • Clear communication and calm under pressure
  • Helping clinics run smoothly (because that affects care)

Global health lens

  • Health outcomes vary by access, environment, and policy
  • Systems matter: transportation, funding, staffing, and infrastructure
  • Equity isn’t a slogan — it’s a practical problem to solve
