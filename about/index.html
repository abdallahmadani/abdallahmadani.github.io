About me

I’m Abdallah Madani, a Syrian-Canadian Honours Life Sciences student at McMaster University. I’m working toward medicine, but the way I think about medicine is very “global” and very “geography”. I care about how health changes by place: access, systems, culture, environment, and what people actually live through.

I like being practical. I want patient-facing work, responsibility, and real outcomes — not just “talking about healthcare” from far away. That’s why I’ve been building experience in clinic settings, while also staying obsessed with the bigger picture: the world map behind every health statistic.

Geography side of me

I’m the type who can stare at a satellite view for 10 seconds and try to identify the country. I’ve done Geotastic pinpoint challenges and placed top 3 against really strong players. I also love flags and learning tiny details about countries.

Creative side

I’m into photography, Photoshop, and video editing. I learned a lot in school, and I kept going on my own. It’s honestly how I decompress while still “building” something.

Where I’ve been

I’ve travelled to: Syria, Jordan, Lebanon, United Arab Emirates, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Germany, Italy, France, Switzerland, Austria, Canada, and the USA. I don’t travel just to “visit” — I pay attention to how cities are built, how people live, and how that connects back to health.
